Soumitra Shukla

Economist, The Burning Glass Institute

I am an Economist at The Burning Glass Institute, a leading nonprofit at the forefront of labor economics research and public discourse.


My work examines how AI is reshaping work and organizations, and how organizations can align talent management practices with labor-market outcomes to expand access to opportunity and upward mobility.


My work has been highlighted by prominent figures such as Steven Pinker, Raghuram Rajan, and featured in popular outlets like Financial Times, Marginal Revolution, The Great Gender Divergence, and Business Insider.


I am from Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, where my parents still live. I currently live in Brookline, MA with my wife Eliza, who is a lawyer, and our golden retriever, Rafa, who obtained her own Juris Dogtor, summa cum laude.
