I am an Economist at The Burning Glass Institute, a leading nonprofit at the forefront of labor economics research and public discourse. I am also an incoming Affiliate at the Stone Center for Research on Wealth and Inequality at the University of Chicago.


I study who gets what jobs, and how technology and social background shape who gets ahead. I work with millions of job postings and worker profiles to understand how AI is reshaping work at scale, from the tasks and skills jobs require to hiring and career progression within organizations. I also study the causes and consequences of socioeconomic inequality in India and the United States, with particular attention to how family background and caste shape access to elite jobs and opportunities for upward mobility.


My work has been highlighted by prominent figures such as Steven Pinker, Raghuram Rajan, and featured in popular outlets like Financial Times, Marginal Revolution, The Great Gender Divergence, and Business Insider.


I am from Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, where my parents still live. I currently live in Brookline, MA with my wife, who is a lawyer, and our golden retriever, Rafa, who obtained her own Juris Dogtor, summa cum laude.
